figure numbering in the paper guide. Figures 2 to 12 are copies of the per-phase pipeline outputs
under `results/` (originals untouched, regenerable via `--from-cache`); figures 1 and 13 were
generated by `experiments/make_paper_figures.py`. Source paths are listed so each is traceable.
